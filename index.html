Why Go Solar

The smart choice for your home and wallet

With energy prices at historic highs, solar is no longer just eco-friendly — it's one of the best financial decisions a homeowner can make.

💷

Cut Your Energy Bills

Most households save between Rs 3,33,000 and Rs 5,18,000 per year after installation. Your system typically pays for itself in 7–9 years — then it's pure profit for 20+ more.

🔋

Store & Use Your Own Power

Pair your panels with a battery and use the solar energy you generate at night. Stop buying expensive grid electricity during peak hours.

📈

Increase Property Value

Homes with solar panels sell for an average of 4% more. Estate agents consistently report solar as one of the most sought-after home improvements.

🌱

Reduce Your Carbon Footprint

A typical 4kW system offsets around 1.6 tonnes of CO₂ per year — the equivalent of not flying London to New York 3 times.

💰

Earn Money Selling Surplus

The Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) pays you for every unit of electricity you export back to the grid. Earn hundreds of pounds extra each year.

🛡️

Protection From Price Rises

Energy prices have risen 300% since 2020. Solar locks in your electricity cost at near-zero for 25+ years, insulating you from future market volatility.
