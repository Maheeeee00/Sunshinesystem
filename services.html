  • Grid import/export tracking with tariff awareness

Smart Analytics & Reporting

Turn raw data into actionable insight. SolarOS's analytics engine crunches your historical data to reveal trends, forecast performance, and calculate your true return on investment down to the penny.

  • 14-day, monthly, and yearly production charts
  • Year-on-year comparison with weather correlation

Alerts

Smart Alerts & Fault Detection

SolarOS monitors your system around the clock and sends instant alerts when something goes wrong — or when performance dips below what it should be. Catch issues before they cost you money.

  • Panel-level fault detection via string analysis
  • Push, email, and SMS notifications
  • Configurable thresholds for temperature, voltage, and output
  • Automatic service ticket creation with your installer
  • Historical alert log with resolution tracking
