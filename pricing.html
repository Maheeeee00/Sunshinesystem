FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a smart meter?

No. SolarOS connects directly to your inverter. A smart meter adds extra import/export data but is not required to get started.

Which inverters are compatible?

We support 40+ brands including SolarEdge, Fronius, Sungrow, SMA, Huawei, and Growatt. Full list on our Services page.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, always. Monthly plans cancel with no penalty at the end of your billing cycle. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata in the first 30 days.

Is my energy data private?

Absolutely. Your data is stored encrypted on UK servers and never sold to third parties. You own your data and can export or delete it at any time.

What's included in the free trial?

The Pro free trial gives you full access to every Pro feature for 30 days — no credit card required. At the end you can subscribe or drop to the free Starter plan.

Do you support battery storage?

Yes. SolarOS monitors and controls compatible battery systems including Tesla Powerwall, Givenergy, and SolarEdge battery — included in Pro and above.
