Most residential installations are completed in a single day. Larger systems or those requiring scaffolding may take 2 days. We'll confirm the exact timeline when we visit for your survey.
In most cases, no. Solar panels are considered 'permitted development' in England, Wales and Scotland, meaning no planning permission is required for most residential roof installations. There are exceptions for listed buildings, conservation areas and some flat roofs. We'll confirm during your survey.
The SEG is a government-backed scheme that requires energy suppliers to pay you for any surplus electricity you export back to the national grid. Rates vary by supplier but typically range from 4p to 15p per unit. MCS certification (which we provide) is required to access SEG payments.
Yes. All of our inverters are 'hybrid-ready', meaning a battery can be easily retrofitted at any time. Costs are the same whether you add it at installation or later. Many of our customers add a battery 1–2 years after their panels when they see how much solar they're generating.
