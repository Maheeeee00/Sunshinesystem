Founded 2010

From two engineers to a 60-person team

Sunshine Solar was founded by James Carter and David Osei — two renewable energy engineers who wanted to bring genuinely high-quality solar installations to UK homeowners at a fair price. No pushy sales, no opaque pricing, no subcontractors.

2010

Founded in Bristol

James and David complete their first 12 residential installations across Bristol and Bath.

2014

MCS Accreditation

We achieve full MCS certification and NICEIC approval, allowing our customers to access Feed-In Tariff payments.

2018

Expanding Nationwide

We open regional offices in Birmingham, Leeds and Manchester, enabling coverage across England and Wales.

2021

Battery Storage Launch

We add home battery storage to our offering, becoming one of the first UK installers to offer Tesla Powerwall alongside own-brand systems.

2026

3,200 Installations & Counting

Over 3,200 completed systems, a 4.9-star average rating and a team of 60 — still led by the same two founders.

Accreditations

Certified, accredited and award-winning

Our certifications mean you can trust that every installation meets the highest safety and quality standards in the industry.

🏅

MCS Certified

Microgeneration Certification Scheme — required for Smart Export Guarantee payments. All our engineers hold individual MCS accreditation.

⚡

NICEIC Approved

National Inspection Council for Electrical Installation Contracting. All electrical work carried out by NICEIC-registered electricians.

🔍

Which? Trusted Trader

Independently vetted and approved by Which? — the UK's most trusted consumer organisation. Endorsed for quality and integrity.

🛡️

TrustMark Registered

Government-endorsed quality scheme for home improvement tradespeople. All work guaranteed under the TrustMark framework.

How We Work

What makes Sunshine Solar different

We're not a sales-led business. We built our reputation on honest advice, high standards and being there long after the installation is complete.

🤝

No-Pressure Sales

Our surveyors are engineers, not salespeople. They'll tell you honestly whether solar is a good fit for your home — and if it isn't, they'll say so. We don't pay commission.

👷

Our Own Engineers, Always

We never use subcontractors. Every installation is carried out by a directly-employed Sunshine Solar engineer — trained to our own standards, insured by us, accountable to us.

💬

Honest, Clear Communication

We tell you exactly what you're getting, what it will cost, and what it will save. No jargon, no false projections, no surprise invoices.

🔧

Long-Term Support

We don't disappear after installation. Our aftercare team is available for the life of your system, and our 5-year workmanship guarantee backs up everything we do.
