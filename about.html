The Beginning

Built out of frustration

Our co-founder James installed a 6 kW solar system on his home in 2020. The inverter came with an app that crashed every morning, showed data 24 hours behind, and had no way to see if a panel was underperforming.

He called his installer. They couldn't see his data remotely. There was a gap — a big one. So he and his co-founder Priya built SolarOS on evenings and weekends. Within six months they had 200 beta users. Within a year, 5,000.

What We Stand For

Our values

🌍

Planet First

Every feature we build is evaluated against one question: does this help the planet? Reducing carbon is not a marketing line — it's our North Star.

🔍

Radical Transparency

We tell customers exactly how their data is used, what our uptime actually was last month, and when we get things wrong. No spin, no small print.

🎯

Obsessive Simplicity

Solar is already complex. SolarOS should make it feel simple. We fight relentlessly for clarity in every screen, every email, every word of copy.

🤝

Customer Champions

Every person who contacts our support team is greeted by a real human who knows solar inside out. We hire for empathy first, technical skill second.

⚡

Bias to Action

We ship fast, learn fast, and fix fast. A good feature released today is worth more than a perfect feature released next quarter.

🧩

Open by Default

Our API is public. Our uptime page is public. Our pricing is public. We believe the solar ecosystem grows faster when we play well with others.
