Our Mission

Energy Freedom for Every Pakistani

Sunsolar was founded on a simple belief: every home and business in Pakistan deserves affordable, reliable electricity — free from loadshedding, free from spiralling WAPDA bills, and powered by the sun we have in abundance.

From a small workshop in Lahore's Gulberg to Pakistan's leading solar EPC company, we've stayed true to that mission: honest pricing, premium equipment and an installation team that treats your roof like their own.
